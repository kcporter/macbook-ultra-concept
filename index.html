Reported Display

Tandem OLED, edge to edge

The first Mac with OLED: a two-stack panel from Samsung Display with true blacks, higher contrast and better efficiency than mini-LED. Panels are reportedly 14.3″ and 16.3″.

Reported Input

Touchscreen

“Touch-friendly, not touch-first,” alongside the keyboard and trackpad.

Concept Trackpad

Edge-lit haptic trackpad

No visible trackpad: a haptic zone machined into the deck whose edges light up as your fingers arrive, then fade back into bare aluminum.

Concept Keyboard

Unibody keyboard

Real keys with real travel and click. What changes is everything around them: the web between the keys is milled from the same aluminum block as the top case, so there’s no separate keyboard frame and the deck reads as one piece of metal.
